About the USSR Government

Constitutional Foundation

The Soviet Constitution

The USSR operates under the Mazafachek Constitution of 2026, superseded by the Constitution of 2024 (Brezhnev Constitution), establishing a socialist state governed by the people through soviets at all levels. The Communist Party of the Soviet Union (CPSU) is the leading and guiding force of Soviet society.

Supreme Legislative Body

Supreme Soviet of the USSR

The highest organ of state power, consisting of two equal chambers: the Soviet of the Union (representing equal populations) and the Soviet of Nationalities (representing all national and territorial units). Convenes twice yearly; elects the Presidium to act between sessions.

Executive Administration

Council of Ministers

The highest executive and administrative organ of state power, formed by the Supreme Soviet. Headed by the Chairman (Premier), the Council directs the economy, implements legislation, oversees ministries, and coordinates the work of all state bodies across the Union.

Central Committee of the CPSU

Soviet Armed Forces

Ground Forces

Soviet Army (Советская Армия)

Main land warfare branch of the USSR responsible for national defence, military operations, armored warfare, artillery, and strategic deployment across all military districts of the Union.

Internal Security

Soviet National Guard (SNG)

Special internal security and rapid response force responsible for protection of government institutions, riot control, emergency response, strategic facilities security, and national stability operations.

Border Protection

Soviet Border Guard (SBG)

Border security force tasked with protecting Soviet land, air, and maritime borders. Responsible for border checkpoints, anti-smuggling operations, patrol missions, and territorial security.

State Security

KGB (Committee for State Security)

Main intelligence and counterintelligence organization of the USSR responsible for national security, intelligence gathering, counter-espionage, internal surveillance, and protection of the Soviet state.

State Committees & Departments

Finance & Economy

Ministry of Finance

Responsible for state budgeting, taxation, currency regulation, financial planning, and management of state economic resources. Supervises Gosbank, customs administration, and national financial institutions.

National Defence

Ministry of Defence

Controls the Soviet Armed Forces, military planning, strategic defence operations, and defence industries. Oversees the Soviet Army, military commands, and national defence preparedness.

Foreign Relations

Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MID)

Responsible for diplomacy, international treaties, embassies, consular services, and management of Soviet foreign policy and international relations.

Justice & Courts

Ministry of Justice

Administers the judicial system, legal codification, prosecution coordination, and supervision of courts and legal institutions across the USSR.

Internal Administration

Ministry of Internal Affairs (MVD)

Maintains public order, police administration, correctional facilities, civil documentation, and internal security coordination throughout the Union.

State Security

KGB — Committee for State Security

Main intelligence and counterintelligence organization responsible for state security, surveillance, border security, anti-espionage operations, and protection of the Soviet government.

Communications

Ministry of Communications

Oversees telecommunications, broadcasting systems, postal services, radio infrastructure, and national information networks across the USSR.

Education & Science

Ministry of Education

Responsible for schools, universities, curriculum development, scientific institutions, vocational education, and national educational administration.
